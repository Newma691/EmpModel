# EmpModel
A detailed discussion and analysis of the Employee Model available in Board Resolution dated 29th September, 2026 and Minutes (28th Oct, 2026) of Medsolu OPC Pvt Ltd.

The recently proposed employee model is one of the largest global economical model which can transform the monetary flow and cashflow system across the globe.

Since this is going to impact the total money generation and flow globally, we can make an analysis of the model both locally within a Geographical region as well as within a Country and Globally.

Key Questions :

(1) How many channels on an average can we imagine within each organisation for the new and old employees  ?

(2) As automated model evolution, what would be the average growth rate in terms of the new and old employee recruitment inclusive of the churn rate of the employees ?

(3) What should be the max threshold for the elimination of the number of newly recruited employee based on extra-ordinary situation ?

(4) How many organisations can RBI allow in a year to evolve with such scheme ?

(6) What would be design of the sector wise (sequential implementation) organisational implementation over time ?

(7) A graphical (GIS based) global/country wise implementational simulations (sequential spread) across the globe over time ?

(8) Identification of the nodes and the intuition behind it.

(9)  A comprehensive picture of the stress level of the cashflow across all important nodes including the overflow and depression of the cashflow for six months to one year ?

(10) A simulation model within the organisation in terms of the number of employees, revenue channels, reduction of the expenditure structure etc.
